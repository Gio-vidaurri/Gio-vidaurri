## 🚀 About

I’m a **Product Design Engineer and Product Builder** with 10+ years of experience designing, building, and scaling digital products across SaaS, e-commerce, HealthTech, FinTech, retail, media, and high-traffic platforms.

I work across the full product lifecycle—from discovery, product strategy, and UX architecture to design systems, prototyping, implementation, automated testing, deployment, analytics, and continuous iteration.

My work sits at the intersection of **product, design, AI, and engineering**. I combine **AI-assisted and agentic workflows** with **Spec-Driven Development (SDD)** and **deterministic-first architectures** to move faster from hypothesis to production while maintaining product judgment, usability, accessibility, security, technical quality, and business alignment.

## 🔧 How I build

**Discover → Define → Specify → Design → Build → Test → Ship → Measure → Iterate**

- Start with the problem, not the requested feature
- Reduce ambiguity through research, data, and rapid experimentation
- Translate business requirements into architecture specs, product systems, and technical decisions
- Move fluidly between Figma, code, AI workflows, data, and production environments
- Use design systems and reusable components to keep design and engineering aligned
- Use AI to accelerate execution—not replace product or engineering judgment
- Validate with automated tests, quality gates, accessibility, security, and real user behavior
- Document architecture, decisions, workflows, and operational knowledge
- Measure outcomes and continuously improve after launch

## 🧠 AI-Assisted Product Engineering

I use AI as part of a disciplined product engineering system—not only as a content or coding assistant.

My workflows include:

- **Spec-Driven Development (SDD)** and architecture contracts before implementation
- **Deterministic-first hybrid pipelines** (reliable code & schema validation for structure; LLMs for synthesis)
- LLM-assisted product research and synthesis
- Context and prompt engineering
- Codex, Cursor, and Claude Code development workflows
- Agentic and multi-agent execution
- MCP-based tool and context integration
- API, queue, and webhook orchestration
- Structured outputs, schema validation (Zod), and deterministic verification
- Human-in-the-loop approval and budget/latency guardrails
- Automated testing, verification, and AI reliability/quality evaluation

*The goal is simple: ship faster without losing control of the product.*
