## 🚀 About

I’m a **Product Design Engineer and Product Builder** with 10+ years of experience designing, building, and scaling digital products across SaaS, e-commerce, HealthTech, FinTech, retail, media, and high-traffic platforms.

I work across the full product lifecycle—from **discovery, product strategy, and UX architecture to design systems, prototyping, implementation, testing, deployment, analytics, and continuous iteration**.

My work increasingly sits at the intersection of **product, design, AI, and engineering**. I use AI-assisted and agentic development workflows to move faster from hypothesis to production while maintaining product judgment, usability, accessibility, security, technical quality, and business alignment.

## 🔧 How I build

**Discover → Define → Design → Build → Test → Ship → Measure → Iterate**

- Start with the problem, not the requested feature
- Reduce ambiguity through research, data, and rapid experimentation
- Translate business requirements into product systems and technical decisions
- Move between Figma, code, AI workflows, data, and production environments
- Use design systems and reusable components to keep design and engineering aligned
- Use AI to accelerate execution—not replace product or engineering judgment
- Validate with automated tests, quality gates, accessibility, security, and real user behavior
- Document architecture, decisions, workflows, and operational knowledge
- Measure outcomes and continuously improve after launch

## 🧠 AI-Assisted Product Engineering

I use AI as part of the product development system—not only as a content or coding assistant.

My workflows include:

- LLM-assisted product research and synthesis
- Context and prompt engineering
- Codex and Cursor development workflows
- Agentic and multi-agent execution
- MCP-based tool and context integration
- API and webhook orchestration
- Structured outputs and deterministic validation
- Human-in-the-loop approval
- Automated testing and verification
- AI cost, latency, reliability, and quality evaluation

The goal is simple: **ship faster without losing control of the product.**
