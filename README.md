# Hi, I'm Gio 👋

**Lead Product Designer · UX Systems · AI Workflows · Vibe Coding · Product Delivery**

Designing and shipping digital products across business, UX, technology, brand, and data.

---

## 🚀 About

I’m a Lead Product Designer with 10+ years of experience building and scaling digital products across SaaS, e-commerce, HealthTech, FinTech, retail, and high-traffic platforms.

I lead products end to end—from discovery, strategy, and UX architecture to design systems, prototyping, implementation, analytics, and continuous optimization.

I use AI-assisted workflows and vibe coding to accelerate research, documentation, validation, automation, and front-end delivery while maintaining product quality, usability, brand consistency, and business alignment.

---

## 🧠 What I do

- 🧭 Product discovery, strategy, prioritization, and delivery
- 🧩 UX architecture, user flows, prototyping, and design systems
- 🎨 Creative direction, visual systems, motion, and brand–product integration
- 🤖 AI-assisted research, documentation, automation, and content workflows
- 💻 Vibe coding and rapid front-end prototyping
- 🏗️ Headless and modular architectures with Astro, Strapi, and Vercel
- 📊 Analytics, technical SEO, CRO, and performance optimization
- 🤝 Cross-functional leadership across business, design, and engineering
